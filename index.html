My dearest, topher

Happy birthday! Thank you for existing and for choosing to grow with me. I know the road ahead won't always be easy. There will be rough days, uncertainties, and moments when things get overwhelming, but I hope we continue to hold on to each other through it all. Let's keep choosing each other and, most importantly, let's take care of each other.

Thank youuuu for taking care of me, lalo na sa mga schoolworks ko. Even when you're already stressed or confused yourself, you never make me feel like I'm too much or that I'm a burden. You still help me without complaining, and I appreciate that more than you know. I know I can't always help you with those kinds of things, but I hope I can be your pahinga, too. A place where you don't have to figure everything out, where you can just breathe and let go of your worries for a while.

You make my worries feel lighter. With you, I don't always have to think about everything or have all the answers. Sometimes, I can just exist and let myself be taken care of. I hope I can give you that same comfort, too.

I also love it when you share your favorite things with me, your hobbies, and even the little niche things you're interested in. I love getting to know the parts of you that you don't always get to show everyone. It makes me happy that you let me into your little world. I hope you never get tired of sharing those things with me. I want to know what makes you excited, the things you could talk about for hours, and even the little things you think are too random to share. I love listening to you, especially when it's about the things you love.

And mahal ko, don't ever forget to choose what's best for you, too. I hope you never lose yourself in the process of loving others. Give yourself the same love, patience, and kindness you so freely give. You deserve to be taken care of, understood, and loved just as much.

I'm grateful that I get to share this journey with you. We don't have to have everything figured out. We can take our time, grow at our own pace, and learn along the way. I hope we get to experience more of life together, discover more about each other, and continue finding comfort in one another.

Happy birthday, my love. Thank you for being here. I hope life is as gentle with you as I wish to be. I love you. ♡
